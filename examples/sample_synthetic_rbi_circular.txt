Illustrative AI Governance Circular
Reserve Bank of India
Synthetic example — not an official RBI publication and not suitable for compliance decisions.

Circular No: DEMO/RBI/AI/2026/01
Published on 20/10/2026
Effective from 01/04/2027

1. Purpose and scope

This illustrative circular describes example governance expectations for artificial intelligence and machine learning systems used by regulated entities in lending and credit decision processes.

2. AI system inventory

Regulated entities shall maintain a documented inventory of artificial intelligence and machine learning systems used in customer-facing or credit decision workflows. The inventory must identify the business owner, intended use, model risk classification and accountable review function.

3. Human oversight

A regulated entity shall ensure that an appropriately trained employee can review and override a material adverse credit decision generated or materially influenced by an automated system.

4. Prohibited practices

Regulated entities must not use customer personal data for an unrelated model-training purpose without an appropriate legal basis and required customer safeguards.

5. Periodic review

Regulated entities should review the AI system inventory at least once every twelve months and retain evidence of the review.

6. Implementation date

The illustrative requirements in this synthetic example are effective from 01/04/2027.
